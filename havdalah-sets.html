About the Havdalah Sets

What colours are available?

The Havdalah sets are handmade in four colours: black, blue, white and orange. Each set is crafted to order in our studio in Israel.

Are the sets made to order?

Yes. Because each set is handmade to order, delivery takes up to 30 business days and usually much sooner. Small natural variations between pieces are part of the handmade character.

What does shipping cost?

Shipping is 35 NIS within Israel and 45 USD internationally.
