Buyer's Guide

Bar and Bat Mitzvah Gifts from Israel

A first piece of Judaica of their own, made by hand in Israel, to keep for life.

A bar or bat mitzvah gift that lasts is one they will use at their own table one day. These are the gifts we make for it in our family studio in Israel.

Browse the Shop Ask us on WhatsApp

The short answer

  • A Kiddush cup of their own is the classic bar mitzvah gift, and it suits a bat mitzvah too.
  • A smaller pair of Shabbat candlesticks suits a bat mitzvah, for girls who light their own candles.
  • A shofar engraved with a symbol and their name makes a gift with a story.
  • A Havdalah set in their favourite colour is a gift for the whole family.
  • Synagogues and communities can give a matching gift to every bar and bat mitzvah of the year.

Why a piece of Judaica?

A bar or bat mitzvah marks the start of keeping the commandments as an adult. A Kiddush cup, candlesticks or a shofar of their own connects the day to what they will do every week, and a handmade piece from Israel is one they are likely to keep.

A Kiddush cup of their own

Choose a colour they love. Our tall glass cups, about 21 cm, stand out on the table; shorter cups, about 9.5 cm, are easier to hold. Every cup has a 925 silver-plated or gold-plated rim, and a matching plate makes it a complete set.

Candlesticks for a bat mitzvah

For girls who light their own Shabbat candles, a smaller pair fits a bedroom shelf or a corner of the table. Our 925 silver-plated and gold-plated pairs come in S (14-18 cm) and M (19-22 cm) as well as L.

A shofar with their name

Our custom ram's horn and kudu shofars are decorated by hand in 925 silver and engraved with a symbol of your choice and an optional inscription in Hebrew or English, such as their name and the date. Custom pieces usually take 2-4 weeks from the day we agree on the design.

A gift from the community

Synagogues and communities can order one design for every bar and bat mitzvah of the year, so each child receives the same gift. Message us with the number of gifts and the date you need them.

Business and community gifts

Every piece is made to order in Israel. Allow up to 14 business days before dispatch, and order a few weeks early before a holiday. Shipping is ₪35 within Israel and $45 anywhere else, or collect from our gallery in Karnei Shomron.

Questions

Bar and Bat Mitzvah Gift Questions

What is a good bar mitzvah gift from Israel?

A Kiddush cup of his own is the classic choice. A shofar engraved with his name or a Havdalah set in his favourite colour are good alternatives.

What is a good bat mitzvah gift?

A smaller pair of Shabbat candlesticks, a Kiddush cup or a Havdalah set in her favourite colour.

Can a shofar be engraved with a name?

Yes. Our custom shofars carry an engraved symbol of your choice and an optional inscription in Hebrew or English.

How far ahead should I order?

Ready designs take up to 14 business days in the studio before dispatch, and custom engraved pieces usually 2-4 weeks from the day we agree on the design. Add delivery time, and order earlier if the gift is going abroad.

Can our synagogue order the same gift for every bar and bat mitzvah?

Yes. Message us with the number of gifts and when you need them, and we will suggest a design.

Can I collect the gift in person?

Yes. In Israel you can choose collection from our gallery in Karnei Shomron at checkout, with no shipping charge.
