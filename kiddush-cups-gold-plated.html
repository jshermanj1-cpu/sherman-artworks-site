About the Kiddush Cups

What are the Kiddush cups made of?

Each cup is handmade to order in Israel, in glass or ceramic, with a 925 silver-plated rim. The glass cups come in several colours, including a design carrying the Bore Pri Hagefen blessing.

Are the gold-plated kiddush cups solid gold?

No. They are handmade glass finished with gold plating, which gives the look and weight of gold at a lower price than solid gold.

How do I care for the silver-plated rim?

Hand wash the cup and wipe the 925 silver-plated rim with a soft dry cloth. Do not put it in the dishwasher and avoid liquid silver polish, which can wear the plating.
