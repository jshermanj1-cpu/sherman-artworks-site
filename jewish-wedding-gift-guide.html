Jewish Wedding Gifts They Will Use Every Week

Shabbat candlesticks, a Kiddush cup, a Havdalah set or a mezuzah case for the couple's first home.

Judaica given at a wedding comes out every Shabbat and holiday for decades. Here is how to choose a piece, from the family that makes them by hand in Israel.

The short answer

  • Shabbat candlesticks are the classic wedding gift: in many families the bride receives her first pair before the wedding.
  • A Kiddush cup with its matching plate is ready for the couple's first Friday night at home.
  • A Havdalah set completes the Shabbat table, from the candles on Friday to Havdalah on Saturday night.
  • A mezuzah case suits the front door of their first home. The parchment scroll is bought separately from a scribe.
  • Giving together? Choose pieces in one colour and finish for a matching Shabbat table.

Why Judaica makes a lasting wedding gift

Most wedding gifts are used up or put away. Candlesticks, a Kiddush cup and a Havdalah set are used every week, and they often stay in the family for a generation. A handmade piece also carries the giver's choice of colour and finish, so it feels personal.

Shabbat candlesticks

In many families the bride receives her first pair of Shabbat candlesticks before the wedding and lights them every week after. Our pairs come in 925 silver-plated and gold-plated glass in three heights, and in artisanal all-glass designs. A matching tray catches the wax and protects the tablecloth.

A Kiddush cup and plate

A Kiddush cup with its matching glass plate is a complete gift for the couple's Friday night Kiddush. Some guests give two cups. Our cups are handmade glass or ceramic with a 925 silver-plated or gold-plated rim, and one tall design carries the Bore Pri HaGafen blessing.

A Havdalah set

A Havdalah set gives the couple everything for the ceremony that ends Shabbat: a cup, a spice box and a candle holder on a matching tray. It pairs well with candlesticks in the same colour.

Havdalah setsHow to choose a Havdalah set

A mezuzah for their first home

A mezuzah case for the front door marks the couple's first home together. Our cases are kudu, ram or oryx horn plated in 925 silver, and clear glass. The case does not include the parchment scroll, which is bought separately from a qualified scribe.

Mezuzah cases

Giving together

If friends or family are giving together, choose several pieces in one colour and finish: candlesticks, a Kiddush cup and a Havdalah set make a matching Shabbat table. We make them in many of the same colours.

Every piece is made to order in Israel. Allow up to 14 business days before dispatch, and order a few weeks early before a holiday. Shipping is ₪35 within Israel and $45 anywhere else, or collect from our gallery in Karnei Shomron.

Wedding Gift Questions

What is a traditional Jewish wedding gift?

Judaica for the couple's home: Shabbat candlesticks, a Kiddush cup, a Havdalah set or a mezuzah case. They are used every week and often stay in the family for years.

Is it a custom to give the bride candlesticks?

Yes. In many families the bride receives Shabbat candlesticks before the wedding, often from the groom or his family, and lights them every Friday after the wedding.

How far ahead should I order a wedding gift?

Each piece is made to order, with up to 14 business days in the studio before dispatch, then delivery. Order at least a month before the wedding, and earlier if it is going abroad.

Can several people give one gift together?

Yes. Choose candlesticks, a Kiddush cup and a Havdalah set in one colour and finish for a matching set. Message us and we will help you put it together.

Can you ship the gift straight to the couple?

Yes. Enter the couple's address as the delivery address at checkout. Shipping is ₪35 within Israel and $45 anywhere else.

Can I collect a wedding gift in person?

Yes. In Israel you can choose collection from our gallery in Karnei Shomron at checkout, with no shipping charge.
