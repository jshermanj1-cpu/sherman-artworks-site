Before You Order

Choosing Your Candlesticks

What sizes do the candlesticks come in?

The candlesticks are sold as a pair in three heights: S (14-18 cm), M (19-22 cm) and L (23-25 cm). A taller L pair stands out as a centrepiece on a large Shabbat table, while S and M suit a smaller table or a shelf. Every pair is handmade glass, made to order in our studio in Israel.

Are the gold-plated candlesticks solid gold?

No. They are handmade glass finished with gold plating, which gives the look and weight of gold at a lower price than solid gold.

How long does an order take and what does shipping cost?

Each pair is made to order, with delivery within up to 30 business days and usually much sooner. Shipping is 35 NIS within Israel and 45 USD internationally.
