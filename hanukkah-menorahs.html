About the Hanukkah Menorahs

What shapes, colours and finishes are available?

There are three shapes: half-moon, rectangular and trapezoid. The glass comes in seven colours: pearl white, blue, clear, emerald green, light blue, terracotta and turquoise. The sockets and base come in silver-tone or gold-tone, and every shape, colour and finish is the same price.

How big are the menorahs?

All three shapes are 33 cm long, 27 cm high and 3 cm deep (about 13 by 10.6 by 1.2 inches), made of double-sided handmade glass.

Is the menorah plated?

No. The menorah is glass, and its sockets and base have a silver-tone or gold-tone finish rather than plating.

What does shipping cost?

Shipping is 35 NIS within Israel and 45 USD internationally.
