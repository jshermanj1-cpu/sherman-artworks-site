Before You Order

About Trays and Bowls

What is in this collection?

The collection includes handmade glass bowls, 925 silver-plated trays, and matching plates for Kiddush cups. Every piece is made to order in our studio in Israel.

Can a Kiddush plate be bought with a cup?

Yes. A Kiddush cup plate is 301 NIS when added to a matching cup, instead of 401 NIS on its own, a 25% saving.

How do I care for a 925 silver-plated tray?

Wipe the tray with a soft dry cloth and keep it dry. Avoid liquid silver polish and the dishwasher, both of which can damage the 925 plating.
